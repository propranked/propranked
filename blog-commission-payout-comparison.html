Prop Firm Commission & Payout Comparison 2026: Who Pays You Most?

Published March 25, 2026 | 10 min read

When evaluating prop firms, most traders focus on evaluation cost and rules. But commission structure is equally important - it determines how much of your profits you actually keep. A firm with lower evaluation costs but 50/50 commission might pay you less over time than a firm with higher entry fees but 90% payouts. Let's break down the real numbers.

Understanding Commission Structures

Prop firms use different commission models. Understanding each type is crucial for calculating true earnings potential:

1. Fixed Percentage Split

You keep a fixed percentage (e.g., 80/20 split means you keep 80%). Most firms use this model. The percentage may vary by account tier and performance history.

2. Tiered Commission

Your payout percentage increases with profitability or tenure. For example: 50% on first $5,000 profit, 70% on $5,001-$15,000, 90% above that. This incentivizes larger profits.

3. Account-Based Commission

Larger accounts get better rates. Micro accounts might be 60/40, while premium accounts are 80/20. Encourages traders to scale.

4. Performance-Based Commission

Commission improves with consistent monthly profits. Your percentage gradually increases as you prove reliability.

Detailed Payout Analysis by Firm

TopStep: The Payout Leader

TopStep offers 100% commission on your first withdrawal, then 90% on subsequent withdrawals. This is industry-leading and reflects their confidence in trader profitability. If you're profitable immediately, TopStep maximizes your first check.

Example: First month profit of $2,000 = you keep $2,000 (100%). Second month profit of $1,500 = you keep $1,350 (90%).

FTMO: Consistent High Payouts

FTMO locks in 80-90% depending on account size. Larger accounts get 90%, smaller get 80%. This consistency is attractive for predictable earnings calculations. No surprises or tiering surprises.

Example: $10,000 account with 2% profit ($200) and 85% payout = you keep $170.

The5ers: Performance-Based Climbing

The5ers starts at 50/50 (you keep 50%) but scales to 80% for elite traders. The progression is based on consistent profitability over months. This rewards dedicated, profitable traders with increasingly better terms.

Starter: 50% commission
Intermediate: 60% commission
Advanced: 70% commission
Elite: 80% commission

Earn2Trade: Real Account Advantage

Earn2Trade uses real brokerages instead of proprietary accounts, allowing direct market access. Commissions are typically 80%+ with potential for higher splits. The real account aspect means direct exchange and clearing house trading.

E8 Markets: Zero Risk, Real Payouts

E8 Markets offers simulator challenges with real payouts. They've paid $70+ million since 2021. Commission varies but focuses on accessibility - even new traders can earn real money from simulated profits.

Hidden Fees That Reduce Payouts

Commission percentage isn't the whole story. Watch for these additional costs:

Common Hidden Costs

Withdrawal Fees: Some firms charge $20-50 per withdrawal. Factor this into your payout calculation.
Account Management Fees: Monthly maintenance costs on larger accounts (rare but exists).
Inactivity Fees: Penalties if you don't trade enough. Usually $50-100/month.
Extension Fees: Extra costs if you need more time on evaluation.
Data Fees: Some firms charge for premium data subscriptions.

Payout Speed & Methods

Your commission percentage matters less if you can't access your money. Compare withdrawal speeds:

Scaling Commission: The Long Game

Most prop firms offer better commission rates when you scale to larger accounts. Typical scaling paths:

  • Pass evaluation on $50K account
  • Trade profitably for 2-3 months
  • Request scale to $100K account
  • Higher tier often means higher commission percentage
  • Continue scaling with consistent profitability

Example: The5ers 50% on $50K → 60% on $100K → 70% on $250K. Your per-trade earnings increase both from larger size and better commission.
