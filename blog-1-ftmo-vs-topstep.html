FTMO vs TopStep: Which Prop Firm is Better for You?

Published March 24, 2026 | 8 min read

Two of the biggest names in prop trading are FTMO and TopStep. Both have thousands of funded traders, but they serve different markets and trading styles. In this comprehensive comparison, we'll break down what makes each unique and help you decide which is right for you.

Quick Overview

FTMO

Market: Forex
Cost: $155 one-time
Evaluation: 2-step
Payout: 80-90%
Best For: Forex traders, European traders

TopStep

Market: Futures (ES, NQ, YM)
Cost: $49/month subscription
Evaluation: Monthly subscription model
Payout: 100% then 90%
Best For: Futures traders, subscription preference

Evaluation Process: FTMO

FTMO uses a 2-step evaluation process. You start with Phase 1 where you trade with a set drawdown limit. If you pass, you move to Phase 2 where you trade with the actual funded account rules.

Most traders find FTMO's evaluation challenging but fair. The 2-step process ensures traders are serious and skilled before receiving funding.

Evaluation Process: TopStep

TopStep works on a subscription model rather than a traditional evaluation. You pay $49/month to trade their simulator, and once you hit certain milestones, you can apply for a funded account.

This approach is more flexible - there's no strict time limit like FTMO's 30-day windows, but you need consistency and discipline to reach milestones.

Markets & Instruments

FTMO: Forex only. You can trade major pairs, minors, and exotics across 5 sessions. Large pip potential but requires good risk management.

TopStep: Micro and standard futures. ES (S&P 500), NQ (Nasdaq), YM (Dow), RTY (Russell 2000). Higher capital potential but requires larger capital allocation.

Payout & Profitability

FTMO: 80-90% payout depending on your account size. Withdrawals available within a few days. Consistent performer with transparent rules.

TopStep: 100% first withdrawal, then 90% on future withdrawals. Monthly subscription cost means you need profit faster to break even.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose FTMO if:

- You trade forex exclusively
- You prefer a clear 2-step evaluation timeline
- You want a one-time $155 payment with no monthly fees
- You're based in Europe (FTMO is very popular there)
- You like trading 5+ sessions per day

Choose TopStep if:

- You trade US futures (ES, NQ, YM)
- You prefer a subscription-based approach
- You want flexibility with no hard evaluation deadlines
- You can sustain $49/month in trading costs
- You like the microfutures learning curve

The Bottom Line

FTMO and TopStep are both legitimate, well-established prop firms. The choice depends on your market preference. FTMO dominates forex, TopStep dominates US futures. Pick based on what you trade, not on which firm is "better" - they're both excellent choices in their respective markets.
