2026 Prop Firm Pricing Comparison: Which Firms Cost Less?

Published March 25, 2026 | 10 min read

One of the first questions traders ask when evaluating prop firms is: how much does this cost? The answer varies significantly across the industry. Some firms charge one-time evaluation fees, others use monthly subscriptions, and some offer free challenges. Understanding the true cost of trading with each firm is crucial to making an informed decision.

The Cost Breakdown

When evaluating prop firm costs, you need to consider several factors beyond the initial fee. These include monthly subscription costs, evaluation extensions, challenge retakes, account maintenance, and potential hidden fees. Some firms offer promotional discounts that significantly reduce initial costs, while others maintain consistent pricing year-round.

Budget-Friendly Options: Under $200

For traders with a tight budget, FTMO stands out as one of the most affordable entry points at just $155 for a full evaluation. This one-time fee gives you 30 days to prove your trading skills. No monthly recurring costs, no hidden fees - just a straightforward evaluation process.

Earn2Trade's Current Deal

Earn2Trade is currently running a 50% off promotion on their TCP and GAU subscriptions. Combined with their focus on real accounts and crypto payouts, they're positioning themselves as an attractive alternative for traders seeking value. With over 6,400 traders funded in 2025 and 4.7 TrustScore, they've built credibility in a competitive market.

No-Cost Entry: E8 Markets

E8 Markets takes a unique approach with zero-risk, zero-deposit simulated capital challenges. You build skill with their simulated capital and earn real payouts - no money required upfront. This eliminates financial risk but requires you to prove consistent profitability before accessing real trading capital.

Subscription-Based Models

TopStep charges $49 monthly for access to their training platform and simulator. This is best suited for traders who plan to spend months learning and preparing. The advantage is flexibility - no hard evaluation deadline. The disadvantage is that costs accumulate quickly if you're unsuccessful.

Cost Example: If you take 6 months to achieve TopStep funding, you'll have paid $294 before making your first trade. Compare this to FTMO's flat $155 fee, and the importance of evaluation efficiency becomes clear.

Variable Pricing & Account-Based Models

Firms like The5ers and FundedNext offer multiple account sizes with different pricing tiers. Generally, smaller accounts (micro) cost less but offer lower profit potential. Larger accounts cost more upfront but provide higher earning capacity. Your choice depends on your risk tolerance and capital goals.

Promotional Offers in March 2026

The prop trading industry is highly competitive, and promotions are common. Apex Trader Funding is currently running an 85% discount promotion, which could dramatically reduce evaluation costs for their services. These time-limited offers are worth monitoring if you're flexible with your firm choice.

The Hidden Costs to Consider

1. Evaluation Extensions

Most firms charge extra if you don't complete evaluation within the initial timeframe. Budget an additional $50-150 for potential extensions if you need more time.

2. Retake Fees

Failed an evaluation? Some firms charge 50-100% of the original fee to retake. This can quickly add up, making your true cost higher than the advertised price.

3. Account Limits & Maximums

To maximize profit potential, traders often need to scale to larger accounts. These typically have higher account fees and profit-sharing percentages that reduce your payout ratio.

4. Withdrawal Fees

A few firms charge withdrawal fees or require minimum withdrawal amounts. Always check the fine print before committing.

ROI Calculation: Cost vs. Potential Earnings

The cheapest firm isn't always the best value. Consider this example: FTMO at $155 might seem cheaper than TopStep at $49/month until you factor in commission percentages. If FTMO takes you 3 months to profit while TopStep funds you in 1 month, TopStep's total cost ($49) is actually lower, plus you start earning sooner.

Calculate your true cost by considering:

  • Initial evaluation fee
  • Time to completion (for subscription models)
  • Commission/profit share on withdrawals
  • Account management fees (if any)
  • Potential retake costs

Final Verdict: Best Value Prop Firms 2026

Best Overall Value: FTMO ($155 one-time) remains the gold standard for affordable prop trading. Quick evaluation, no recurring costs, and transparent rules.

Best for Zero Risk: E8 Markets eliminates financial risk with zero-deposit challenges. Perfect if you're completely new to the game.

Best Promotional Deal: Earn2Trade's 50% off sale and Apex Trader Funding's 85% discount are currently the most aggressive offers. These won't last long.

Best Flexibility: TopStep's subscription model works well if you need time to learn and prefer no hard deadlines.

Action Plan: How to Choose

1. Determine your budget (one-time vs. monthly)
2. Calculate time-to-profitability for each option
3. Check for active promotions
4. Review the evaluation rules and pass rates
5. Factor in commission percentages on your expected profit
6. Sign up with the firm that offers the best overall value for your situation
