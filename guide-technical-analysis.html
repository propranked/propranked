Technical Analysis for Prop Traders: Complete Guide

Technical analysis is essential for prop trading. Whether you're trading futures, forex, or equities, understanding price action and chart patterns will help you identify high-probability setups and pass your prop firm evaluation.

Support and Resistance Levels

Support and resistance are the foundation of technical analysis. These are price levels where the market has historically bounced or reversed.

Support: A price level where buying interest emerges, preventing further downside

Resistance: A price level where selling pressure emerges, preventing further upside

Professional traders use support and resistance to:

  • Place stop losses below support or above resistance
  • Set profit targets at next resistance or support
  • Identify breakout opportunities
  • Determine entry and exit points

Trend Analysis

Trends are your friend in trading. The direction of the trend should influence your bias:

  • Uptrend: Series of higher highs and higher lows — bias to buying
  • Downtrend: Series of lower highs and lower lows — bias to selling
  • Sideways: No clear direction — avoid large positions

Always trade with the trend, not against it. Trading against the trend significantly reduces your win rate.

Key Chart Patterns

Breakouts: When price breaks above resistance or below support with volume, often signals a strong move. Many prop traders specialize in breakout trading.

Pullbacks: Price retraces part of a move before resuming. Common entry points for trend traders.

Head and Shoulders: Reversal pattern showing three peaks, with the middle peak highest. Often precedes downtrends.

Double Tops/Bottoms: Price tests the same level twice and reverses. Indicates weakness and potential reversals.

Moving Averages

Moving averages smooth out price action and reveal trends:

  • 20-day MA: Short-term trend
  • 50-day MA: Medium-term trend
  • 200-day MA: Long-term trend

When price is above all three moving averages, the market is in a strong uptrend. When below all three, it's in a strong downtrend.

Volume Analysis

Volume confirms the strength of price moves. High volume on breakouts suggests strong conviction. Low volume breakouts often fail.

Strong Signal: Price breaks resistance + high volume = likely continuation

Weak Signal: Price breaks resistance + low volume = likely to reverse

Applying Technical Analysis to Evaluations

When trading prop firm evaluations, focus on:

  1. Trading major support/resistance levels
  2. Using moving averages to confirm trend
  3. Taking breakouts with volume confirmation
  4. Avoiding choppy, sideways markets
  5. Keeping stops close to invalidation levels

Bottom Line

Technical analysis works because millions of traders use it, creating self-fulfilling prophecies at key price levels. Master support/resistance, trend analysis, and chart patterns, and you'll have the foundation for successful trading at any prop firm.
