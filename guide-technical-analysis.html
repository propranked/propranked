Technical Analysis for Prop Traders

Identify high-probability setups using support/resistance, trend analysis, chart patterns and moving averages.

Technical analysis is essential for prop trading. Whether you're trading futures, forex, or equities, understanding price action and chart patterns will help you identify high-probability setups and pass your evaluation.

Support and Resistance Levels

Support: A price level where buying interest emerges, preventing further downside.

Resistance: A price level where selling pressure emerges, preventing further upside.

Professional traders use support and resistance to place stop losses, set profit targets, identify breakout opportunities, and determine precise entry and exit points.

Trend Analysis

  • Uptrend: Series of higher highs and higher lows — bias to long positions
  • Downtrend: Series of lower highs and lower lows — bias to short positions
  • Sideways: No clear direction — avoid large positions until a breakout occurs

Always trade with the trend, not against it. Counter-trend trading significantly reduces your win rate and is especially dangerous during evaluations.

Key Chart Patterns

Breakouts: When price breaks above resistance or below support, often signaling a strong move. Many prop traders specialize in breakout strategies.

Pullbacks: Price retraces part of a move before resuming the trend. Common and reliable entry points for trend traders.

Double Tops/Bottoms: Price tests the same level twice and reverses. Indicates potential reversal of the current trend.

Moving Averages

  • 20-period MA: Short-term trend — useful for scalpers and day traders
  • 50-period MA: Medium-term trend — most widely watched by institutional traders
  • 200-period MA: Long-term trend — defines bull/bear market structure

When price is above all three moving averages, the market is in a strong uptrend. When below all three, it's in a strong downtrend. Confluence with support/resistance makes these levels extremely powerful.

Volume Analysis

Strong Signal: Price breaks resistance + high volume = likely continuation

Weak Signal: Price breaks resistance + low volume = likely to reverse

Applying Technical Analysis in Evaluations

  1. Trade major support/resistance levels only — avoid the noise in between
  2. Use moving averages to confirm trend direction before entry
  3. Take breakouts with volume confirmation for higher probability
  4. Avoid choppy, sideways markets — patience is key
  5. Keep stop losses close to the invalidation level of your setup

Bottom Line

Technical analysis works because millions of traders use it, creating self-fulfilling price reactions at key levels. Master support/resistance, trend analysis, and chart patterns, and you'll have a solid foundation for profitable trading at any prop firm.
