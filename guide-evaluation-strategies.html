How to Pass Prop Firm Evaluations

Proven strategies from traders who passed FTMO, TopStep, Apex, FundedNext and more.

Passing a prop firm evaluation is a significant accomplishment. Thousands of traders attempt evaluations every month, but only a fraction pass. This guide covers the strategies that consistently work.

Understand Your Evaluation Requirements

✓ Daily Loss Limit: Maximum you can lose in one day
✓ Maximum Drawdown: Maximum cumulative loss allowed
✓ Profit Target: How much profit you need to hit
✓ Minimum Trading Days: How many days you must trade

Read the rules before risking a single dollar. Most traders who fail do so because they didn't fully understand the constraints they were operating under.

Start Slow and Build Confidence

  1. Start with small positions — 1–2 micro-contracts or minimal lots
  2. Focus on consistency, not chasing profits
  3. Track every trade in a journal
  4. Increase size only after 10+ successful trades
  5. Never increase size to "make back" losses

Write a Trading Plan Before You Start

  • What markets you'll trade (ES, NQ, YM, EUR/USD, etc.)
  • Your trading hours and best time windows
  • Entry and exit rules — written, not improvised
  • Position size for each scenario
  • Your personal daily loss limit (stop trading after X loss)

The Psychology of Passing

Emotions kill evaluations. When you're close to hitting your profit target, it's tempting to take excessive risk. Stick to your plan and let wins accumulate naturally.

❌ Over-trading to hit target faster
❌ Taking excessive risk on a single trade
❌ Revenge trading after a loss
❌ Ignoring or moving stop losses

At 50% Profit, Shift Strategy

Once you've reached 50% of your profit target, you're in a strong position. Now the priority changes:

  • Slightly reduce position size to protect gains
  • Focus on consistency, not adding more profit
  • Avoid the temptation to over-trade
  • Let time work in your favor — slow and steady wins

Bottom Line

Passing prop firm evaluations is about discipline, not exceptional skill. Follow your plan, manage risk with precision, control emotions, and you'll significantly improve your pass rate.
