The prop trading industry moves fast. New promotions, platform updates, and competitive announcements happen constantly. Here's your roundup of the biggest changes and announcements affecting prop traders in March 2026.

1-Step FTMO Challenge Expanding AvailabilityNEW

FTMO is expanding its 1-Step Challenge availability to more traders. The 1-Step model accelerates evaluation for experienced traders, skipping Phase 1 and going directly to Phase 2. This reduces time-to-funding for qualified applicants while maintaining evaluation standards.

Eligibility: Typically requires proven trading history or prior FTMO experience. New traders start with the traditional 2-Step process.

Industry Trends to Watch in Q2 2026

1. Crypto Integration Increasing

More prop firms are adding crypto futures and crypto withdrawal options. The5ers and Earn2Trade lead this charge with direct crypto payouts. Expect other firms to follow as traders demand this flexibility.

2. Commission Compression

Competition is squeezing commission spreads. TopStep's 100% first withdrawal was revolutionary; other firms are matching or exceeding this to stay competitive. Better payouts benefit traders.

3. Affiliate & Referral Programs Growing

Prop firms are expanding affiliate programs, allowing traders to earn by referring others. This creates additional income streams and demonstrates firm confidence in their product quality.

4. AI & Technology Integration

Platform improvements and AI tools are becoming standard. Real-time feedback, trading analytics, and performance optimization tools are differentiators in 2026.

5. Stricter Regulations

Watch for increased regulatory scrutiny in certain jurisdictions. Established firms (TopStep, The5ers, FTMO) with solid compliance records will benefit as regulations tighten.
