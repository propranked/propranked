The5ers Evaluation Rules & Features: Complete Guide for 2026

Published March 25, 2026 | 9 min read

The5ers has been a staple in the prop trading industry since 2016, and with their recent 10-year anniversary celebration, they're reinforcing their position as one of the most trader-friendly funding platforms. If you're considering The5ers, understanding their evaluation rules and features is crucial. Let's break down everything you need to know.

Company Overview

The5ers is an experienced prop trading firm founded in 2016 with a focus on developing trading careers through education and real funding. They've funded over 4,000+ traders and maintain an excellent TrustScore of 4.8 with 21,657 reviews. Their tagline "Turning Your Passion Into A Trading Career" resonates with traders seeking legitimate career opportunities in prop trading.

Account Sizes & Funding Structure

The5ers offers multiple account sizes starting from micro accounts up to $4,000,000 in simulated capital. This range accommodates traders at all experience levels, from beginners learning with small accounts to professional traders managing multi-million dollar portfolios.

Micro Accounts

Smallest account tier, ideal for beginners testing the platform and learning evaluation rules without significant financial pressure.

Standard Accounts

Most popular tier for intermediate traders with proven track records seeking to scale their trading capital.

Premium Accounts

High-tier accounts for experienced traders with larger profit expectations and capital management experience.

Elite Accounts

Exclusive tier for top performers seeking the highest capital allocation and best commission splits.

Key Evaluation Rules

Profit Target

Your primary objective in evaluation is achieving The5ers' profit target. The specific percentage varies by account size, but ranges from 8% to 12% of account balance. Once you hit this target with consistent trading, you progress toward funded status.

Drawdown Limits

Maximum drawdown rules protect both you and The5ers. These limits vary by account tier but typically range from 8-12% of starting capital. Daily drawdown limits are also enforced, preventing any single bad day from eliminating your account.

Daily Loss Limits

You cannot lose more than a specific percentage in a single trading day. This rule teaches proper risk management and prevents emotional revenge trading. Most traders find this protective rather than restrictive.

Minimum Trading Days

The5ers requires you to trade a minimum number of days during evaluation. This ensures you're truly active rather than lucky on one profitable day. Expect to trade at least 10-12 days of the evaluation period.

This diversification allows traders to specialize or diversify across multiple market types within a single account.

Commission & Profit Split Structure

Once you're funded, The5ers operates on a profit-sharing model. Your commission percentage depends on your account tier and performance history:

Typical Commission Structure

Starter Traders: 50/50 split (The5ers takes 50%, you keep 50%)
Intermediate Traders: 60/40 split (you keep 60%)
Elite Traders: 70/30 split (you keep 70%)
Top Performers: Up to 80/20 split possible with multi-account management

Scaling & Tier Progression

The5ers encourages traders to scale up through consistent profitability. Successful traders can request account increases beyond their initial capital allocation. This progression path rewards proven performance with higher capital and better commission ratios.

Scaling Requirements

  • Consistent profitability for at least 2-3 months
  • Pass a scaling evaluation challenge (shorter timeframe, lower profit target)
  • No recent drawdown warnings or rule violations
  • Demonstrated risk management and discipline
