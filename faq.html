Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about prop trading firms and PropRanked.

What is a prop trading firm?
A prop trading firm (proprietary trading firm) provides traders with capital to trade financial markets. Traders evaluate their skills, get funded if they pass, and then split profits with the firm.
How much does it cost to join a prop firm?
Costs vary from $49/month to $250+ depending on the firm. Most charge a one-time evaluation fee between $95-$300. Check our comparison table for specific pricing.
Can I really make money with prop firms?
Yes, but it requires discipline and trading skill. You'll split profits with the firm (typically 60-95% to you). Success depends on your trading strategy and risk management.
What's the difference between one-step and two-step evaluations?
One-step: Pass one evaluation, get funded. Two-step: Pass initial evaluation, then another evaluation with the funded account before full funding. Two-step is more rigorous.
Which prop firm is best for beginners?
We recommend starting with firms that have clear rules and good support like FTMO, TopStep, or FundedNext. Check our reviews for detailed comparisons.
Do prop firms trade your own money?
No. Prop firms provide capital for you to trade. You don't deposit your own money (though some require initial evaluation fees). Profits are split between you and the firm.
What markets can I trade with prop firms?
Most prop firms offer futures, forex, or both. Some newer firms also support crypto. Check individual firm reviews for specific market access.
How are PropRanked reviews created?
Our reviews are independent and trader-verified. We analyze pricing, payout rules, evaluation difficulty, community feedback, and real user experiences to rank each firm fairly.
Is PropRanked affiliated with these prop firms?
We earn affiliate commissions when you sign up through our links. This supports our work but doesn't influence our reviews. All opinions are independent and unbiased.
How often do you update the reviews?
We update pricing and rules monthly. Our last major update was March 2026. Subscribe to our blog for updates when firms change their offerings.
